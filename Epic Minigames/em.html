Epic Minigames is a popular roblox round-game style experience developed by TypicalType, with assistance of TheSteelEagle, Azarctic and worthyshining kazoo. In the game, players participate in games through a series of short rounds, allowing them to experience a variety of minigames. If the player wins, they are awarded both 10 coins and 1 point allowing them to advanced through levels - each earning the player a set of 100+ coins and additional perks.

Types of minigames

On March 11th 2022, Epic Minigames released a key system what catagory a minigame belonged to. There are 6 types of minigames:

Survival minigames

Requires the player to survive the minigame's obstacles and challenges for a set period of time. The icon representing this type of minigame is a volcano.

Team minigames

Where players are grouped into 2-4 teams, players are randomly chosen into either a blue or red team. These teams compete against each other by achieving the minigame's goal. The icon representing this type of minigame is a red sword and a blue sword crossed together.

Versus minigames

When players compete against each other to best achieve the minigame's goal. The icon representing this type of minigame is the text 'VS' .

Racing minigames

Requires the player to complete the minigame's objective the fastest. The icon representing this type of minigame is a black and white checkered flag.

Controller minigames

When the player is randomly selected to 'control' the minigame's obstacles - which the rest of the players must avoid under a set time to win. The controller's main goal is to eliminate all players, if all players are eliminated the controller wins (vice versa if the players survive). The icon representing this type of minigame is a joystick.

Luck minigames

When the minigame primarily relies on luck instead of skill to win. The icon representing this type of minigame is a clover.
