The Legend of Zelda: Breath of The Wild & Tears of The Kingdom

The history of the royal family of Hyrule is also the history of the Calamity Ganon — a primal evil that has endured over the ages. A prophecy told of Calamity Ganon’s return, and the people of Hyrule turned to their legends for a solution. They excavated ancient sites and discovered dormant machines called the Divine Beasts. Four Champions—Mipha, Urbosa, Revali, and Daruk—were chosen to pilot these powerful weapons, and leading them was Zelda, the princess of Hyrule. Protecting her was a loyal knight named Link—the hero chosen by the legendary sword that seals the darkness.

Despite their best efforts, Calamity Ganon was resurrected and turned the machines against the kingdom of Hyrule. In the end, the four Champions and the King of Hyrule perished, Link was gravely wounded, and Hyrule crumbled to ashes. To protect Link from certain death, Zelda awakened her sacred powers and sealed away Calamity Ganon temporarily. Link was taken to the Shrine of Resurrection to recover.

A few years after the events of Breath of the Wild, Link and Zelda are exploring a secret passage hidden beneath Hyrule Castle. Although King Rhoam had warned Zelda in the past that not even the royal family was to go there, she believes that what lies beneath is connected to the strange phenomena spreading throughout Hyrule, referred to as ‘Gloom’. As they travel, they find ancient ruins that Zelda identifies as being of Zonai origin.

They eventually find a room with large murals. The first one depicts the Zonai descending to Hyrule, floating, with Hylians bowing down at the bottom of the mural and seven teardrop-shaped stones (Secret Stones) floating around them. This causes Zelda to realize that the ‘gods’ that descended from the heavens to found the royal family and Hyrule must have been Zonai. The next shows a Zonai and a Hylian joining hands with two secret stones floating above them, and Hylians seemingly rejoicing in all the corners of the mural. Next to that, there is an image of the same Hylian who joined hands with the Zonai in the last mural floating seemingly prone as tendrils of dark material swirl around her, its source a large humanoid being.

Above that being is the Secret Stone that the Hylian once held. The last (visible) mural shows the humanoid now much larger, with monstrous features, surrounded by monsters. This seemed to be depicting a great war against a being usually referred to as "The Demon King", the stories of which had been passed down by the royal family.

The Time Line(s)

The timeline had previously existed in a highly confidential document that had been concieved to the public in 2002 with the release of Wind Waker.

In 2007, Eiji Aonuma confirmed the existence of two parallel timelines branching from Ocarina Of Time. These two timelines are now referenced as The Child Timeline, and The Adult Timeline. Hyrule Historia released a thrid branch of the timeline called the "Fallen Hero Timeline", resolving the timeline contradictions with respect to titles *before* Ocarina Of Time. The proceeding three way split is known as Hylia and the Hero of Time. Where Breath of The Wild and Tears of The Kingdom are placed in the timeline is vague and up to interperatation.

Hylia and The Hero of Time

Before time began, there was only a formless void known as the Still World, where Null would erase anything that tried to form. The three golden Godesses - Nayru - the Goddess of Wisdom, Din - the Goddess of Power, and Farore - The Goddess of Courage. Nayru - the Goddess of Wisdom, bestowed order upon the world. Farore - the Goddess of courage, created life. And Din - the Goddess of Power, created the Earth. As a result of their efforts, Null was imprisoned. However, Null attempted to create rifts so that the world would return to void, but the Goddesses summoned Tris to mend the rifts.

With their labour complete, the Goddesses left the heavens. They left behind the Triforce - an all powerful which could grant the holder of the triforce their heart's desire.
