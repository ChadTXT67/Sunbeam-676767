The Legend of Zelda: Breath of The Wild

The history of the royal family of Hyrule is also the history of the Calamity Ganon — a primal evil that has endured over the ages. A prophecy told of Calamity Ganon’s return, and the people of Hyrule turned to their legends for a solution. They excavated ancient sites and discovered dormant machines called the Divine Beasts. Four Champions—Mipha, Urbosa, Revali, and Daruk—were chosen to pilot these powerful weapons, and leading them was Zelda, the princess of Hyrule. Protecting her was a loyal knight named Link—the hero chosen by the legendary sword that seals the darkness. Despite their best efforts, Calamity Ganon was resurrected and turned the machines against the kingdom of Hyrule. In the end, the four Champions and the King of Hyrule perished, Link was gravely wounded, and Hyrule crumbled to ashes. To protect Link from certain death, Zelda awakened her sacred powers and sealed away Calamity Ganon temporarily. Link was taken to the Shrine of Resurrection to recover.
