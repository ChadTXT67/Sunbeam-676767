Synopsis

Kingdom Hearts is a story of light, darkness and friendship, featuring a boy named Sora weilding
a magical weapon known as a keyblade to journey through worlds from Disney or original Square
Enix worlds. Kingdom hearts mainly explores themes of human connection, the battle of light and
darkness and the struggles of growing up; so while it is targetted for children, its depiction
of the complexity of human nature makes playing Kingdom Hearts an enjoyable experience for
people of all age ranges
